## ✨ Features & Technology
- **3D Royal Maratha Wax Seal Envelope:** Interactive 3D unfolding envelope opening with authentic wax seal break and letter slide-out.
- **Three.js WebGL 3D Ambient Universe:** Real-time 3D golden & bougainvillea petals fluttering with organic turbulence, glowing fireflies, and camera parallax responsive to mouse & mobile gyroscope.
- **Interactive 360° 3D Interlocking Wedding Rings:** Drag and spin 3D 24k Yellow Gold and Rose Gold diamond solitaire rings in 360° with specular light reflections.
- **Interactive Scratch-to-Reveal Muhurat Card:** HTML5 canvas gold-scratch card with ceremonial confetti burst upon 50% reveal.
- **Procedural Shehnai/Flute Music Player:** Web Audio API procedural Indian classical raga synthesizer (zero external audio file dependencies).
- **Live Countdown Timer:** Real-time countdown to Thursday, 26 Nov 2026 13:05:00 IST.
- **1-Click Google Maps & WhatsApp Wishes:** Direct navigation to venue location and pre-formatted Marathi WhatsApp wishes.
- **Editorial High-Fashion Typography:** Curated Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel, and Rozha One Devanagari typography.
