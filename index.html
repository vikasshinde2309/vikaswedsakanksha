From Mumbai / Navi Mumbai

Drive via Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH-66) or via Panvel → Pen → Nagothane → Roha. Approx. 2.5 to 3 hours scenic drive through the Konkan countryside.

Approx. 115 km
🏔️

From Pune

Take the Mumbai-Pune Expressway → exit at Khopoli → via Pali (Ballaleshwar temple road) → Roha. Approx. 2.5 to 3 hours journey.

Approx. 125 km
🚆

By Train (Roha Station)

Roha Railway Station (ROHA) is well-connected on Central Railway & Konkan Railway. Local autos and cabs are readily available from Roha station to Vani (15 mins).

15 mins from Roha Stn

Accommodations & Stays

Comfortable hotel and resort options are available within Roha town and nearby Nagothane/Kolad belt for guests arriving from afar. Please connect with our family coordinators for assistance.
